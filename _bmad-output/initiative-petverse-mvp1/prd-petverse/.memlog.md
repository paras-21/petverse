- (decision) Success target: 40%+ of new players still playing after 7 days
- (decision) Counter-metrics: bots taking the pot, whales taking most of the pot, new players quitting in week 1 after being robbed, too many pet deaths without return
- (decision) Release 1 is done when all 16 features work and are stable with no major bugs
- (decision) 1v1 duel: winner gains rank and takes some of the loser's points
