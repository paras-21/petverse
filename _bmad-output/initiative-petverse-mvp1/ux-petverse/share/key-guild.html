Key screens 5: Guild

The Guild tab is home for your team of up to 10. The top card says who you are ("Carrot Club", 8 of 10 members, you lead) with one clear Invite a friend button. The Guild War card shows Sunday's series in big type: who you face, the score, and your next match with your partner. The Gold prize is shown in dollars first. The second phone is the live match board: 5 slots, first to 3 wins. Alternate state: a player in no guild sees "Join a guild or start one" and a short list of guilds near their level.

How Guild War works

Every Sunday your guild meets a guild of a similar level. You play 5 matches, 2 pets on each side. The first guild to win 3 matches wins, and everyone in it shares the Gold prize.
