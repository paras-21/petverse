Key screens 5: Death Match

Death Match is the weekend event on the Play tab (Saturday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 UTC). An adult pet enters with 3 lives. Each win adds 1 to the Survivor streak and pays 2x a ranked duel's points times the streak, up to 5x. Each loss costs 1 life. At 0 lives the pet passes away, so the risk is said plainly, more than once, and in kind words. Before entering you type your pet's name to confirm. During the run, "Leave now and keep rewards" is always a big button, never hidden. On the last life the app stops and asks "Leave safely?". Lives come back 1 every 7 days of good care. A Co-Pet needs both owners to agree. Desktop shows the weekend lobby: choose a pet, see the reward ladder, the leaderboard and the Hall of Fame.
