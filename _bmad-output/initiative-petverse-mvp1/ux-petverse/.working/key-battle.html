Key screens 2: Battle

The Battle tab lists pets near your level. Every row says what you can win and how likely it is before you tap ("Win +620 · 54% chance"). A revenge row sits on top when someone robbed you; it skips the wait. The fight plays by itself: two pets, two health bars, playful hit labels. The result card shows the points in big type. Alternate state: a loss, said gently, with the wait shown on the disabled button. The desktop frame shows the list while the 30-minute wait is running.
