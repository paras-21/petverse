Key screens 4: Shop and claim

The Shop tab has three parts in one segmented control: Store, Pass and Market. Every price leads with dollars in big type; the PETV token amount sits small underneath. Tapping a cosmetic tries it on your pet before you buy (Mochi wears the propeller cap). Rarity is a word plus 1 to 4 stars, so it never relies on colour. The Pass sells like Fren Pet's: one dollar price, short benefit rows that say "instead of", one button. The Market only lists rare and better items and says the 5% fee up front. Load-bearing overlay: the claim sheet. It says the dollar amount first, warns plainly that points go back to 0, explains the pot in one line, and gives a calm way out: "Not now, keep growing".
