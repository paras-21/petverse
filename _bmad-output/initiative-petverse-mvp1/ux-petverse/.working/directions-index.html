Petverse: 4 design directions for the pet home screen

Same screen, same content (Mochi, a level 6 adult bunny), and the same 3D pets in every direction. Each frame shows a phone home, a phone state variant and the 1280 desktop. Click a name to open it full size.

A. Cozy: Sunday picnic

Warm paper, cocoa, sage and peach. Arched storybook stage and soft pills. Calm, care-first.

B. Bold: Arcade cabinet

Navy borders, hard shadows, monospace caps, yellow, red and teal. Dense and competitive.

C. Premium: Night garden

Green-black with brass, serif numbers and hairlines. Quiet, collectible, value-first.

D. Candy: Sticker book

Bubblegum stickers, blob stage, glossy pills, emoji. Loud, playful and social.
