Bold 6. Retro Console

A pocket handheld from 1989, rebuilt with modern chunky UI: the screen uses only the four classic olive greens, the device shell is grey plastic, and corners are stair-stepped pixels. The one extra colour is the berry of the A and B buttons, used only for danger, so action, danger and gain still separate cleanly. Nostalgic and calm-but-gamey: for players who grew up on handhelds.
