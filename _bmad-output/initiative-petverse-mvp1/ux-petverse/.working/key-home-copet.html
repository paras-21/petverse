Key screens 5: Home and Co-Pet

Home is where the day starts. A pet switcher at the top lists your own pets and your Co-Pets; a Co-Pet chip carries the friend's small avatar, and a dashed egg adds a pet. Under it: the pet on its blob stage, the feed timer, points (big, lime) and your share of the pot (dollars first, ETH small), the robbed alert with Revenge, the daily wheel next to the attack wait, and the login streak. Co-Pet view: the same Home, but for a pet you share with Riya: both faces, who fed today, the Together bonus, the shared streak, the 50/50 split and the three things that need you both to agree. Alternate state: the invite sheet that starts a Co-Pet.
