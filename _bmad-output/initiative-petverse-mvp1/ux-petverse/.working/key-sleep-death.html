Key screens 5: Sleep and passing away

Care has a clear clock. When Mochi gets hungry the timer turns orange and Home leads with one big Feed button. If nobody feeds Mochi for 3 days, Mochi falls asleep: the stage dims, it gets no points or pot share, and Mochi can be robbed. Waking is free, but the cost is shown before you tap (10% of points for each day asleep), next to how many days are left. After 7 days asleep Mochi passes away. That screen is calm and kind: a Tombstone badge to keep, a plain line about the pot, and a free new egg. Alerts reach players outside the app by browser push and Telegram.
